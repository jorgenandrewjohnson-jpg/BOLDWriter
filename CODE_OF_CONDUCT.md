# Code of Conduct

BOLD Writer welcomes authors, designers, testers, and developers of all experience levels. Participation in issues, pull requests, reviews, and project discussions should be respectful and focused on helping people create reliable books.

Be considerate of different backgrounds and skill levels. Critique ideas and code with specific evidence; avoid personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, and unwanted disclosure of private information. Respect an author's ownership of their manuscript and artwork.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, pause a discussion, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate and explained where doing so does not expose someone else's private information.

To report conduct concerns privately, use the repository's private maintainer contact once it is configured. Until then, open a minimal issue asking for a private contact channel without naming affected people or sharing sensitive details. Maintainers will review reports confidentially to the extent practical and will not retaliate against good-faith reports.
